### 1. Key Differences

The Armenia implementation follows the default platform behavior, with one addition: a country-specific category of specialists called U-FRIEND.

### 2. U-FRIEND specialists

U-FRIEND is a category of specialists available only in Armenia. U-FRIEND specialists are marked with a “U-FRIEND” label on their profiles and consultation cards, in place of the professional title shown for psychologists.

Consultations with U-FRIEND specialists last 30 minutes. In all other respects – finding and booking a specialist, joining the consultation, the consultation itself and the post-consultation questionnaire – they work the same way as consultations with psychologists (see the Client and Provider manuals).
